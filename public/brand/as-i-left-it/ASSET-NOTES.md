## Layouts and animation

Social PNGs are rendered from editable SVG layouts embedding the generated transparent mascot. The character remains a raster illustration. SVG text and framing are editable; re-rendering on another machine may use a different system font.

The replacement trailers are animated concept explanations, not recorded application behavior. Native canvas motion tells a clutter → review → choice → undo → calm story. All objects and examples are fictional. The score is synthesized original music; captions and transcripts are included. The exported WebM audio is decoded and checked for non-silent PCM, not merely an audio-track header. Movies are click-to-play, with an explicit sound-enabled control.

The mascot's future in-app role is secondary polish tied to real state: returning, holding saved context, active processing, blocked work and completion. It should respect reduced motion and never imply idle model calls, continuous monitoring, sentience or hidden activity.
